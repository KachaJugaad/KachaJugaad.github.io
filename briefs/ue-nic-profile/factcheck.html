Deep dive

How the checking was done, and where it stops

  • Read in full: Chapter 7 of UE Spec 1.1, pages 752–760, including all six figures. Page numbers are the spec's printed numbers, which match the PDF.
  • "Could not find it published" means I searched and found nothing. It does not mean the UE NIC profile cannot exist outside the 764-page spec; the release notes point to separate management documents.
  • Not re-checked here: statements #11 and #12 come from OCP documents without page numbers. Check them against the source before quoting.
  • Kept off the page on purpose: vendor names, customers, prices and performance numbers.

Example

One statement, traced end to end

"UE NIC endpoint manageability shall be prescribed by extending the OCP Ethernet NIC profile."
1Open UE Spec 1.1 at printed page 756, section 7.4, "Redfish for UE Endpoint Manageability". 2The sentence appears there word for word. "Shall" makes it a requirement, not a suggestion. 3Table 7-1 sits just below it: 16 tasks, all Mandatory. None mentions LLR, CBFC or CSIG. 4So the spec points at a UE NIC profile but lists only the OCP baseline. That gap is the brief's whole argument.

Takeaways

What to take from this

  • If the UE NIC profile turns up published, statements #2–#4 change, and I will update the brief.
