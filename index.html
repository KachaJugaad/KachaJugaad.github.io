How I think

The stuff that doesn't
fit on a résumé.

01

"Data exists. Listening is the leverage."

Every system I've walked into had the signals buried somewhere — Maximo logs, production traces, carrier registries. The problem was never missing data. It was that nobody built the thing that turned it into a decision.

02

"Metrics tell you what happened. Products tell you what to do next."

A dashboard showing high idle time isn't a product. A signal telling an operator which asset to move and when — that's a product. The gap between reporting and action is where the PM work actually lives.

03

"Talk to operators before writing specs."

Not users. Not stakeholders. The person doing the job. Discovery means going airside at YVR before the first wireframe. On calls with T-Mobile ops before defining a single metric. The job description lies. The operator doesn't.

04

"Sometimes the job is removing the obstacle."

Canadian developers couldn't test carrier APIs without a procurement agreement. The technology existed. The friction was the product problem. So I built the sandbox. Not every problem needs a feature — some just need someone to get out of the way.

What's next

Open Networking · OVN

Mixed-vendor data center fabric nobody's integrating.

Tariffs hit networking gear 10-36%. Organizations buy what's available — three generations of switches, three config languages. The open-source fabric manager doesn't exist.

Read the full problem brief ↓
  • Auto-discovery normalizing SONiC, Arista EOS, Cisco NX-OS into a single inventory
  • Fabric readiness score — can these devices interop, and where are the gaps?
  • Intent-based config generation handling EVPN default mismatches across vendors
  • Supply chain awareness — EOL devices matched to compatible replacements

Juniper Apstra solved this — then HPE acquired it. SONiC is real ($5B+ market by 2026), but Day 2 ops tooling is immature. OVN handles the virtual layer but has 15 unassigned issues, scaling bottlenecks, and no clean physical network story.

The hardware exists. The open-source NOS exists. Nobody's built the integration layer that makes a mixed-generation fabric usable without 40 network engineers.

Why Canada: Bill C-8 mandates supply chain risk management. 97% of mid-market orgs repatriating for sovereignty. Data centers expanding across Toronto, Calgary, Saskatchewan — all facing the same integration wall.

Defense Edge · Frugal Compute

$38.6B for NORAD and warehouses of old servers. Nobody's connecting the two.

Enterprise hardware gets decommissioned when the warranty expires, not when it stops working. Defense edge needs compute where you can't ship a $400K DGX.

Read the full problem brief ↓
  • Edge inference runtime that auto-profiles hardware and selects the right model quantization — best model the hardware can handle, not the best money can buy
  • Commodity fleet manager for defense edge — discovers and orchestrates mixed-vintage compute into a mesh
  • Sovereign data pipeline — sensor data processed locally, summaries forwarded up-chain. PIPEDA/Protected B by architecture, not policy
  • Hardware lifecycle extension — score decommissioned gear for defense edge reuse: can it run inference? What latency? What power envelope?

A 3-year-old server running a properly quantized model at the edge beats a bleeding-edge GPU 2,000 km away — latency, sovereignty, and availability matter more than raw FLOPS at an Arctic monitoring station.

Canada's defense modernization doesn't have a technology problem. It has an integration problem. Same pattern as every other problem I've worked on.
Career Insurance · AI Agents

Your career is your most expensive asset. Nobody treats it like one.

A $120K salary over 25 years is $3M in lifetime earnings. We insure houses and cars. The earning machine? We cross our fingers.

Read the full problem brief ↓
  • Career insurance — self-funded buffer tied to career risk profile. Proactive, not reactive
  • AI agents managing earning potential — TFSA/RRSP/FHSA optimization plus career stage, skill gaps, certification ROI
  • Universal portfolio — brokerage, credentials, network strength in one view. Blockchain-verified portable reputation
  • Expense-to-investment intelligence — $200/month streaming is consumption; $200/month cloud cert is investment

6-8M self-directed brokerage accounts in Canada. Most strategy is "buy VEQT." 25-30% of skilled immigrants underemployed — $11-15B/year in lost GDP. The bank gives newcomers a $500 credit card and wishes them luck.

The PM who's lived this problem from both sides. The gap between job searches isn't a pause. It's the expensive problem nobody built a product for yet.
